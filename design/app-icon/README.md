# App icon: "Семь звёзд и лучи"

The letter S drawn as a constellation of seven stars, with faint rays to distant stars (links to notes in other constellations).

- `app-icon-ios.svg`: full-bleed 1024 artwork for iOS (the system applies the corner mask).
- `app-icon-mac.svg`: macOS icon on the 1024 grid (824 squircle with shadow); used for 128 px and larger.
- `app-icon-mac-small.svg`: same mark without the faint rays and with bolder strokes; used for 16–64 px, where the rays turn to noise.

The PNGs in `Space App/Space/Space/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset` are rendered from these files.
